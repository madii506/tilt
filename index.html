03how it works

Your bag. A fresh pair. One pot.

  1. 01

    Pick the bag you're down on

    The memecoin that's been bleeding in your wallet. It stops being dead weight and becomes your stake.

  2. 02

    Pick a fresh pump.fun pair

    Any pair from the board, minutes old. The board shows how far it has moved since its first trade.

  3. 03

    Take a side in its pot

    HEDGE says it dumps. HOLD says it doesn't. Every pair gets one pot, and both sides pay into it.

  4. 04

    Settled once

    After 24 hours the pot reads the pair's price straight from its pool. The winning side splits the pot. You get fees on StonkFun.

06rules

The pot, plainly.

how a pot works
One pot per pairEvery fresh pump.fun pair can have one pot. Anyone can join either side while it's open.
Two sidesHEDGE wins if the pair trades well below its first price when the pot settles. HOLD wins if it doesn't.
Settled once24 hours after it opens. The price is read on chain from the pair's own pool, averaged over the last hour, so one dump at the bell can't fake it.
Paid in the potThe winning side splits the whole pot by stake. Nothing to claim from us.
Your bagThe bag you pick is sold for SOL at market, and that SOL is your stake. Most bags you're down on can't be staked as they are.
draft rules · the exact line, fee and window are published before the first pot opens
what can go wrong
It's a betA pot is a wager on one coin. You can lose your whole stake.
Devs know firstA dev can join HEDGE and then rug their own pair. HOLD has to price that in.
One-sided potsIf almost everyone is on one side, winning pays very little.
Not live yetThe board and bag check work today. Pots open once the program is on mainnet and its address is public.
Not adviceNothing here tells you what to buy or sell. Check the pair yourself.
07questions

Straight answers.

What is tilt?

A way to hedge fresh pump.fun pairs. Each pair gets one pot with two sides: HEDGE says it dumps, HOLD says it doesn't. You fund your side with the memecoin you're already down on.

Do new pairs really dump?

Check the board: it shows, live, how many of the newest pairs already trade below their first price. Judge for yourself.

Why use the bag I'm down on?

It's money you've already written off. tilt turns it into a position that pays if the pair you pick dumps.

Where does the data come from?

The board reads GeckoTerminal's feed of new pools on pump.fun. Bag check reads token accounts from a public Solana RPC and prices from Jupiter. Nothing on this site is made up.

Can I hedge right now?

Not yet. Pots need their program on mainnet first. The board and bag check are live today.

What does "a StonkFun layer" mean?

StonkFun is a Solana launchpad where coins can be paired with anything. tilt is built on top of it: StonkFun is the base layer, tilt adds the hedge for pump.fun memecoins, and $TILT launches on StonkFun.

Is tilt part of pump.fun or StonkFun?

No. tilt is an independent layer built on StonkFun. It isn't affiliated with pump.fun or StonkFun.
